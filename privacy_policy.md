# ArrowVelixa Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026

ArrowVelixa is an offline puzzle game designed to provide a fun, relaxing, and enjoyable gaming experience. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how ArrowVelixa handles information when you use the application.

## 1. Information We Collect

ArrowVelixa does not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal information to play the game.

We do not directly collect personal information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Physical address
- Payment information
- Precise location

## 2. Game Progress and Local Storage

ArrowVelixa stores certain information locally on your device to support gameplay functionality.

This information may include:

- Current level and completed levels
- Game scores and achievements
- Sound and music preferences
- Haptic feedback settings
- Notification preferences
- Tutorial completion status

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

## 3. Notifications

ArrowVelixa may request permission to send occasional game reminders.

Notifications are optional and are scheduled locally on your device.

You can enable or disable ArrowVelixa reminders within the application's Settings screen.

You can also manage notification permissions through your device settings.

ArrowVelixa does not use notifications to collect personal information.

## 4. Advertising and Analytics

ArrowVelixa does not currently include:

- Third-party advertisements
- Advertising tracking SDKs
- Third-party analytics SDKs
- Behavioral tracking systems

We do not sell or share personal information with advertisers.

## 5. Third-Party Services

ArrowVelixa uses certain platform services to support its functionality.

These may include:

- **Apple App Store:** For application distribution and updates.
- **Apple In-App Review:** To optionally request ratings and reviews.
- **Apple Notification Services:** For managing notification permissions and displaying local notifications through iOS.

These services are governed by Apple's applicable privacy policies.

For more information, visit:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 6. Children's Privacy

ArrowVelixa does not require children or other users to submit personal information.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe there is a privacy-related concern involving the application, please contact us.

## 7. Data Security

Game progress and preferences are stored locally using your device's application storage.

We do not operate a remote database for collecting or storing player information.

Device backups and other operating-system services may be managed by Apple according to your device settings.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

ArrowVelixa retains locally stored game information while the application remains installed, unless it is cleared through available device or application functionality.

You can delete locally stored application data by removing the application from your device.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Any changes will be reflected in the updated policy, along with a revised effective date.

## 10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support information provided on the ArrowVelixa App Store listing.
